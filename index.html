What should our group agreement cover?

Our contract asks us to agree on how we work together. This page lays out the main topics, the choices on each, and what each choice trades off. Open a topic, compare the options side by side, and mark where you lean. Nothing here is decided.

Before you start

  • Make a summary for yourselfMark the options you lean toward and add notes, then use “See my summary” to copy or print them.
  • This site does not save anythingYour choices are not stored or sent anywhere. They disappear when you close or refresh the page, so copy your summary first.
  • Have a different idea?Message David and he will add it as an option.

Reading the options

  • Gold: where the group is leaning for now. David updates this after the group talks.
  • Blue: the option an AI assistant (Claude) judged most practical under the current contract. One suggestion to react to, not the group’s view.
  • Red: unlikely to meet the contract, or would need a contract change. The contract is not expected to change during its term.
